1. Getting started

SetMaker builds balanced doubles sets for your club's social and ladder sessions. It keeps sit-outs fair, reduces repeated partners and opponents, and lets you adjust matches by hand at any time.

Signing in

  • Log in with the email and password for your club account.
  • If you were invited by your club, open the invitation link, confirm your details and set a password.
  • Forgotten your password? Use Forgot password? on the login page.

Recommended first steps

  1. Add your club's courts and set their priority.
  2. Add your players, including a skill rating for each.
  3. Optionally save a template for sessions you run regularly.
  4. Create a session and generate the first round.

2. Roles & permissions

3. Managing players

The Players page is your club roster. Search by name, then select a player to see their details and notes.

Adding a player

  1. Click + Add Player.
  2. Enter their name and gender, and optionally notes and a court surface preference.
  3. Owners and admins choose a rating from 1 to 10. Staff can leave this blank for an admin to fill in later.
  4. Click Add Player.
A player must have a skill rating before they can be placed in a set. Players without one are left out when a round is generated.

Duplicates, editing and deleting

  • If a player with a similar name already exists, SetMaker warns you before saving so you don't create a duplicate.
  • Use Edit to update a player's details at any time.
  • Delete (owners and admins) deactivates the player. They stop appearing in new sessions, but their past rounds are kept.

4. Managing courts

The Courts page lists every court at your club.

  • Add Court to record a court's number, surface type, cover and priority.
  • Priority decides which courts are filled first. Your best courts should have the highest priority.
  • Use the toggle to mark a court Inactive (for example, during resurfacing). Inactive courts aren't offered when you create a session.

5. Session templates

Templates save the settings you use every week so you don't have to re-enter them. Owners and admins manage them from the Templates page.

A template stores:

  • Match format: Mixed Doubles or Open Play (any gender).
  • Number of rounds, round length, start time and total session length.
  • An optional tea break after a chosen round.
  • Which courts to use. Leave Set as default priority order on to follow the Courts page, or turn it off to choose a custom order for this template.

6. Creating a session

  1. From the Dashboard, click + Create Session.
  2. Enter a session name, date and start time.
  3. Set the number of rounds, round duration, session duration and an optional tea break.
  4. Choose a match format and tick the courts you're using.
  5. Under Attending Players, tick everyone who is playing today.
  6. Click Create & Generate Session. SetMaker saves the session and builds Round 1.
Allow Format Relaxation lets SetMaker adjust the format when numbers don't divide evenly (for example, too few women for full mixed doubles) so everyone keeps playing. Turn it off if the format must be followed strictly.

If you started from a template, its settings are filled in for you. You can still change them for this session only.

7. Running a live session

Open a session from the Dashboard to see each court's teams, who is sitting out, and every round played so far.

Each round

  1. Check the suggested matches. To swap someone, change the player in that slot.
  2. Click Save Changes to make your edits official, or Undo Unsaved Changes to go back.
  3. When play finishes, click Complete Round. Completed rounds are locked.
  4. Click Generate Next Round. SetMaker uses earlier rounds to rotate sit-outs fairly and mix up partners.

Late arrivals and early departures

  • Add Late Player: choose a club player who isn't in the session yet. They're included from the next generated round.
  • Unavailable From Next Round: choose a player who is leaving. They won't be placed in later rounds, and their earlier rounds are kept.

Correcting a completed round

Owners and admins can reopen a completed round to fix a mistake, as long as the next round hasn't been generated yet.

The session is marked complete automatically when its final round is completed.

8. Team members & club details

  • Team Members lists everyone with access to your club and their role.
  • Click + Invite Team Member, enter their name, email and role, then Send Invite. They finish setting up their account from the invitation link.
  • Club Details holds your club's name, contact email, phone and address.

9. Troubleshooting

A player is missing from the generated round.
Check that they have a skill rating, are still active, and haven't been marked unavailable. Late players only join from the next round that's generated after they were added.
"Generation failed" after creating a session.
The session itself was saved. Open it and click Generate Next Round to try again and see the reason. Usually there are too few eligible players for the selected courts.
I can't complete the round.
Save or undo your edits first, and make sure the current (latest) round is selected.
My match edits won't save.
Each player can only appear once per round. Also check that nobody else edited the round at the same time; if so, reload the page and try again.
I can't reopen a round.
Only owners and admins can reopen rounds, and only before the next round has been generated.
I can't set skill ratings, add courts or edit templates.
Those actions are for owners and admins. Ask one of them, or see Roles & permissions.
Still stuck? Contact your club owner or admin. They can check your role and your club's setup.
