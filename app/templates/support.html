1. Getting started

Setify builds balanced doubles sets for your club's social and ladder sessions. It shares sit-outs fairly, keeps teams close in skill, mixes up partners and opponents, and lets you change matches by hand at any time.

Signing in

  • Log in with the email and password for your club account. Tick Remember me to stay logged in on that device for 30 days.
  • If you were invited by your club, open the link in your invitation email. It shows the club, your role and who invited you. Enter your name, choose a password (at least 8 characters) and you're in. Invitation links expire after 7 days.
  • Forgotten your password? Click Forgot password? on the login page and enter your email. We'll email you a reset link that works once and expires after 1 hour.
  • To log out, click your name at the bottom of the sidebar and choose Log Out.

Recommended first steps

  1. Add your club's courts and set their priority.
  2. Add your players one at a time, or import them from a CSV file. Each player needs a skill rating.
  3. Optionally save a template for sessions you run regularly.
  4. Create a session and generate the first round.
  5. Show the QR code so players can see their courts on their phones.

3. Your account

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar and choose My Account (or go to Settings › My Account).

  • Your name: change how you appear to your team.
  • Password: enter your current password and a new one. You stay logged in on this device; any other devices are logged out, and we email you to let you know.
  • Login email: enter the new address and your current password. We send an approval link to your current email first, so nobody using your computer can move your account to their inbox. After you approve it, the new address gets a confirmation link.
  • Confirm your email: if your email shows Not confirmed, click Send confirmation email and open the link. Your account works either way.

4. Managing players

The Players page is your club roster. Each row shows the player's rating (one green dot per point, up to 10), their gender (a blue dot for male, red for female) and their sit-out record.

  • Type in the search box to filter by name.
  • Click Name, Rating, Gender or Sit-outs at the top of the table to sort by it. Click again to reverse the order.
  • Click a player to see their details on the right: sit-outs, who they've played with most, recent sessions and notes.

Adding a player

  1. Click + Add Player.
  2. Enter their name and gender, and optionally notes and a court surface preference.
  3. Owners and admins choose a rating from 1 to 10. Players added by staff start at rating 1; an owner or admin can change it later.
  4. Click Add Player.

Adding a lot of players at once? Import them from a CSV file instead.

A player must have a skill rating before they can be placed in a set. Players without one don't appear under Attending Players when you create a session.

Duplicates, editing and deleting

  • If a player with a similar name already exists, Setify warns you before saving. If they really are a different person, tick Confirm this is a different person and save again.
  • Use Edit to update a player's details at any time.
  • Delete (owners and admins) asks you to confirm, then removes the player from your roster. They stop appearing in new sessions, but the rounds they've already played are kept.

5. Sit-out records

When numbers don't divide evenly, someone has to sit out. If a player asks why they're sitting out, the Players page shows how their sit-outs compare with everyone else's. Everything is based on the last 50 rounds each player attended.

The Sit-outs column

  • 4 / 42 means they sat out 4 of their last 42 rounds.
  • The tag compares that with how many sit-outs were expected for the sessions they came to. Busy days mean more sit-outs for everyone, so a player who comes on busy days is expected to sit out more.
    • Even (green): within 1 sit-out of expected.
    • Over (orange): more than expected. Setify takes this into account, so they're less likely to sit out next.
    • Under (blue): fewer than expected.

The full-screen breakdown

Click a player, then the expand icon at the top right of their card. The table is replaced by a breakdown you can turn around and show the player:

  • A plain-English summary, e.g. "Avery has sat out 5 times in their last 50 rounds. About 3.8 were expected…"
  • Rounds attended, sat out, expected sit-outs, sit-out balance and sit-out rate against the club average.
  • Round by round: one square per round, grey for played and orange for sat out. Striped squares are sit-outs caused by a staff change to the round, not by Setify.
  • Sit-outs per session against what was expected, and a chart of every player's sit-out rate with this player highlighted.
  • Most played with: the players they've shared a court with most, as one bar each. Green is rounds as their partner, orange is rounds as their opponent. Shows the top 5, or up to 10.

Click ← Back to all players, the icon again, or press Esc to return to the table.

6. Importing players from CSV

Owners and admins can add many players at once from a spreadsheet. Save it as a CSV (UTF-8) file, then on the Players page drag it onto the Drop a CSV of players here box, or click browse to choose it. The import starts straight away.

Columns

The first row must be the column names. They can be in any order, and capital letters don't matter.

Column Required Accepted values
Name Yes The player's name
Gender Yes M, F, Male or Female
Rating Yes A whole number from 1 to 10
Notes No Any text
Court Preference No Clay, Hard, Grass or Synthetic

Example

Name,Gender,Rating,Notes,Court Preference
Alex Smith,M,6,,Hard
Fiona Lee,Female,7,Prefers morning sessions,Clay

After the import

  • A message at the top of the page shows how many players were imported and how many rows were skipped.
  • A row is skipped if the name matches or closely matches an existing player (or an earlier row in the file), or if a required value is missing or not accepted.
  • Skipped rows aren't added. Fix them in your spreadsheet and import just those rows again, or add them by hand.
Files must be .csv, under 2 MB and no more than 1,000 players. If Excel offers several CSV options, choose CSV UTF-8.

7. Managing courts

The Courts page lists every court at your club.

  • Add Court to record a court's number, surface type, cover and priority.
  • Priority decides which courts are filled first. Courts with a lower number are filled first, so give your best court priority 0 or 1.
  • Each court shows a coloured surface tag: Clay, Hard, Grass or Synthetic.
  • Use the toggle to mark a court Inactive (for example, during resurfacing). Inactive courts aren't offered when you create a session or template.
  • Click Edit to change a court's details.

8. Session templates

Templates save the settings you use every week so you don't have to re-enter them. Owners and admins create and edit them from the Templates page.

A template stores:

  • Format: Mixed Doubles, Same-Gender Doubles or Open Play (any gender).
  • Pairing Mode: Balanced Skill, Round Robin or Avoid Repeats. See How Setify picks the sets.
  • Number of rounds, round length, start time and total session length.
  • An optional tea break after a chosen round.
  • Which courts to use. Leave Set as default priority order on to follow the Courts page, or turn it off to choose a custom set of courts for this template.

To use one, click Use Template on its card. Anyone can do this, including staff. You'll go to Create Session with the template's settings already filled in. Use Edit at the top right of the card to change it.

9. Creating a session

  1. From the Dashboard or Sessions page, click + Create Session, or click Use Template on a template.
  2. Enter a session name, date and start time, the number of rounds and the round length.
  3. Choose a Match Format and a Pairing Mode (hover the i next to each mode for a reminder), and tick the courts you're using.
  4. Set the session duration, an optional tea break, and whether to Allow Format Relaxation.
  5. Under Attending Players, start typing a name and pick from the suggestions (use ↑ ↓ and Enter, or click). Each player appears as a tag above the box; click × to remove one. Click All players to see everyone, men first then women, and click names to add or remove them.
  6. Click Create & Generate Session. Setify saves the session, builds Round 1 and opens the live session.
Allow Format Relaxation lets Setify adjust the format when numbers don't divide evenly (for example, too few women for full mixed doubles) so everyone keeps playing. Turn it off if the format must be followed strictly.

If you started from a template, you can still change any setting for this session only. The template itself isn't changed.

10. How Setify picks the sets

Who sits out

  1. Nobody sits out twice in a session until everyone there has sat out once.
  2. Nobody sits out two rounds in a row unless there's no other way.
  3. When there's a choice, the player with the lowest sit-out record over their last 50 rounds sits out first. New players are treated as average, so they aren't made to catch up.

Pairing modes

Mode What comes first Skill gaps allowed Best for
Balanced Skill (default) Even, close matches; then avoids repeat partners and opponents where it can Team totals and partners within 2, ideally 1 Most social sessions
Round Robin Mixing everyone up so players meet as many different people as possible Within 2 Social days where variety matters
Avoid Repeats Never repeating a partner or opponent if it can be avoided Up to 4 Large groups who play together often

In every mode Setify keeps partners close in skill, so a strong player isn't paired with a much weaker one just to make the totals even.

Repeat partners and opponents only count within today's session. Every session starts fresh, so playing with someone last week doesn't stop you being paired with them today. Sit-out fairness is the exception: it looks back over each player's last 50 rounds.

11. Running a live session

The live session page shows the player, court and match counts, each court's teams, who is sitting out, and a button for every round so far. Click a round button to look back at an earlier round.

Changing the matches

  • Drag a player onto another player to swap them, or click one player and then another. This works across courts and with the Sitting Out list. Press Esc to cancel.
  • Players you've moved are highlighted in yellow until you save.
  • Click Save Changes to make your edits official, or Undo Unsaved Changes to go back.
  • Click Show Skill to see each player's rating and each court's team totals (e.g. 10 vs 9), which update as you swap. The total is green for a gap of 0–1, grey for 2 and orange for 3 or more. Click Hide Skill before turning the screen to players. Setify remembers your choice.

Each round

  1. Check the matches and make any swaps.
  2. When play finishes, click Complete Round. Completed rounds are locked.
  3. Click Generate Next Round.

Players arriving or leaving

  • Add Player: type the name of a club player who isn't in the session yet and pick them. They join from the next round generated.
  • Remove Player: type the name of a player who's leaving and confirm. They finish the current round and sit out from the next one. Their earlier rounds are kept.
  • Save or undo any swaps first, because adding or removing a player reloads the page.

Ending the session

  1. Save or undo any edits to the current round.
  2. Click the orange End Session button at the top of the page and confirm.
  3. The current round is completed, the session is marked complete, and you're taken to History. All saved matches are kept.

You don't have to play every planned round. End Session works at any point.

12. Sharing matches with a QR code

Every live session page shows your club's QR code under the Sitting Out list, labelled Scan for Session Results. Players scan it with their phone camera to see where they're playing, without logging in.

What players see

  • Your club name, the session name and the current round number.
  • Each court with its Team A and Team B players, and who is sitting out.
  • The page refreshes every 15 seconds, so players can leave it open between rounds.

Good to know

  • The code never changes. It always shows your club's most recently created session, so you can print it once and put it up at the club.
  • Players only see saved matches. Click Save Changes after swapping players so the public page updates.
  • Before Round 1 is generated, players see Waiting for matches to be generated.
Anyone with the QR code or its link can see player names and court assignments. Skill ratings, notes and contact details are never shown on the public page.

13. Sessions & history

  • The Sessions page and the Dashboard list your sessions as cards showing the date, time, a coloured format tag, player and round counts and a status (green Active, blue Upcoming, grey Completed). Click anywhere on a card to open it.
  • The History page lists every completed session. Search by name, or filter by date or Session Type. Clear Filters resets them.
  • Click a session name or View Results to see every round that was played. History shows 10 sessions per page.

14. Team members & club details

Inviting a team member

  1. On Settings › Team Members, click + Invite Team Member.
  2. Enter their name and email, and choose their role.
  3. Click Send Invite. Setify emails them an invitation link.
  4. They open the link, set a password and are logged in. The link expires after 7 days.

If they didn't get the email or the link has expired, go to Accounts, click Revoke on their pending invitation, then invite them again.

Managing accounts

  • The Accounts page (owners and admins) lists every member and pending invitation.
  • To change someone's role, pick it in the dropdown and click Save Role.
  • Remove ends a member's access straight away and shows them as removed. Their email is freed, so they can be invited again or use it to register their own club. Sessions and rounds they worked on are kept.
  • Revoke cancels a pending invitation. Admins can only manage staff.

Club details

The Club Details tab shows your club's name, which appears across the app and on the public QR code page. Owners and admins can change it and click Save Changes.

15. Troubleshooting

A player is asking why they keep sitting out.
Open the Players page, click them, then the expand icon. The breakdown compares their sit-outs with what was expected and with the rest of the club. See Sit-out records.
A player is missing from Attending Players or the generated round.
Check that they have a skill rating, are still active, and haven't been removed from the session. Players added during a session only join from the next round that's generated.
"Generation failed" after creating a session.
The session itself was saved. Click Generate Next Round on the live session page to try again and see the reason. Usually there are too few eligible players for the selected courts.
I can't complete the round, add or remove a player, or end the session.
Save or undo your swaps first, and make sure the current (latest) round is selected. If End Session is greyed out, the session has already ended; find it on the History page.
My match edits won't save.
Someone else may have edited the round at the same time. Reload the page and try again.
My CSV import skipped some rows.
Those players are probably already on your roster (or have a very similar name), or a row has a missing name, a gender other than M/F/Male/Female, or a rating outside 1 to 10. See Importing players from CSV.
My CSV file was rejected.
The file must end in .csv, be under 2 MB, have no more than 1,000 rows, include Name, Gender and Rating columns, and be saved as CSV UTF-8. Each column name can only appear once.
Players see an old session on the QR page.
The public page shows your most recently created session and only saved matches. Check you've saved your changes, and wait up to 15 seconds for it to refresh.
I didn't get an invitation, password reset or confirmation email.
Check your spam folder first. Reset links expire after 1 hour and invitations after 7 days, so ask for a new one if it's old. If emails never arrive, ask whoever runs Setify for your club to check its email settings.
I was logged out unexpectedly.
Changing or resetting your password logs out your other devices. Log in again with your new password. Without Remember me, you're also logged out when the browser closes.
I removed someone by mistake.
Invite them again from Settings › Team Members using the same email. They'll set a new password when they accept.
I can't set skill ratings, import players, add courts or edit templates.
Those actions are for owners and admins. Ask one of them, or see Roles & permissions.
Still stuck? Contact your club owner or admin. They can check your role and your club's setup.
